    Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

    One playthrough is enough. No difficulty setting, no mission replay, no DLC, no multiplayer. Difficulty about 4/10; roughly 200 to 300+ hours (PowerPyx ~200 h; Push Square 300+ h). Never use cheats — cheats disable trophies and your save. Keep 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave: save at the start and end of every chapter (10 saves) and rotate the other 5 in free-roam.

    Stage 1 — Story & first pass 60–80 h

    Play the story (5 chapters). Along the way:

    • Do not skip the Chapter 1 meditation at Jason’s house — the one possibly missable trophy (see Positive Mental Attitude).
    • Scan every new vehicle with WAiNK (target 200 of 277 base models for 100%).
    • Study new animals through the scope (target 100 of 255).
    • Start optional quests as they appear (target 100 for 100% — there are 200–300+, so take it easy).
    • Character questlines: Andy (Lucia, Ch. 3), Kenton (Ch. 1), Billy (after Ch. 2 “Coffee Break”), Randy (Ch. 3), Tyrone (Jason, Ch. 3), Bret (Jason, after Ch. 2 “Coffee Break”) — all stay open after the story.
    • Save manually at the start and end of each chapter. There is no mission replay.

    Trophies: all 12 story chapter/mission trophies, the final-mission gold, and most early activities.

    Stage 2 — The 100% checklist ~100 h

    After the story, the checklist opens (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion). Work it in this order:

    • Robberies, all of them: 39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 7 payday loans, 8 vaults, 34 of 35 smash ’n’ grabs, 50 of 91 stick-ups, plus fencing $100k narcotics / $100k jewellery / $250k marked bills / $200k vehicles.
    • Must be complete: 33 underwater discoveries, 42 shooting-range golds, 42 drone time trials, 15 Target Deck entries for Bret.
    • Partial targets: 15 card sets, 100 WAiNK scans, 30 magazines/books/comics, 15 meditations, 100 flamingos, 2 playlists, 70 styles, 30 unique race wins, 10 gold race challenges, 4 arcade games, 5 pool opponents, 2 mini-golf courses, 90 basketball hoops, 300 stunts, 70 of 80 challenges, 200 vehicles, 50 standard weapons, 100 animals.

    Trophies: A Long Road (gold, 100%), Cunning Stunts, The Shootist, Top Fun, Earn Cash Not Kudos, Don’t Touch My Deck, You WAiNKer!, Maximum Withdrawal, and most of the rest.

    Stage 3 — Strangers & clean-up 10–20 h

    Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands (they do not count toward 100%), the remaining meditations, and any misc trophies still open — Hot Together, Retail Therapy, Gunslinger and the rest mostly pop naturally during Stage 2. Check the tracker trophy by trophy; the platinum pops with the last one.

    Trophies: Strangers & Freaks, the two reported-but-unconfirmed misc trophies (The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin), then Vice Got a Price!

    What the numbers still argue about

    • Gold race challenges for 100%: PowerPyx says 10 of 13; Push Square says 30. Follow the in-game checklist number.
    • The final mission’s name: guides do not agree, so this guide names none — “the final mission (Chapter 5)” only.
    • Strangers & Freaks: only 4 strands are confirmed to count (Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete); more strangers may still be undiscovered.
    • The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin: methods reported by PowerPyx, but Keengamer notes both are still unconfirmed.
    • Card map: only 58 of 480 card locations are publicly known; the 480 total is reported, not fully confirmed.
    • Sneakthief’s hidden status: most guides list it hidden; Games.gg lists it visible. This guide follows the majority.
